FAQ

Before You Book.

Practical answers for the consultation journey.

What happens during a private consultation?

We discuss occasion, outfit direction, references, colour, details and fitting so the next step is clear.

Can I customise an outfit?

Yes. Final fabric, colour, embroidery, silhouette, fit and pricing are confirmed during consultation.

Can I bring a reference image?

Yes. A reference, mood or idea can start the design conversation.

How is price confirmed?

Pricing depends on design, materials and craft. Confirmed during consultation.

How do I request an appointment?

Use the form or contact options. Preferred date/time is a request; boutique confirms the slot.
